A little monkey.
A big soft spot for music.

Meet Bobo: your cuddly, curious songbook companion. Three ways to draw the same little personality.

The little hugger

Our starting point

Soft belly, big ears, a curious head tilt. The guitar is almost as big as Bobo. Warm, expressive, and easy to love.

The pick thief

Bigger ears, a sideways smile, and the missing pick tucked into a curly tail. A bolder, cheekier little companion.

The songbook companion

Fine ink lines, little fur marks, and a favorite blue cushion. The gentlest, most picture-book version of Bobo.

The direction: the hugger’s soft proportions, with the pick thief’s curious expressions. Keep the music clear; let Bobo bring the warmth.
